# SproutPal

**Plan the garden your plot can actually grow.**

SproutPal is a garden planning and management app. You draw your plot — its size, orientation, soil and the things already on it — and SproutPal helps you decide what to plant, where, and why.

## Vision

Most gardening advice is generic: "full sun", "well-drained soil", "plant in spring". But every garden is different. A plant that thrives on one side of the house may struggle on the other.

SproutPal starts from *your* plot:

- **Know your space.** Map the plot and its fixed elements, and see how sunlight moves across it through the seasons.
- **Plant with confidence.** Get recommendations that match each spot's light and soil to your goals, with a clear explanation for every suggestion and a warning when something won't fit.
- **Keep it growing.** Follow a garden calendar, track tasks and keep a journal of what worked.

The goal is simple: fewer failed plantings, and a garden that gets better every year.
