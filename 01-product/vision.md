# Vision

## Problem

Gardening knowledge is everywhere and nowhere. It is scattered across plant labels, books, blogs, forums and videos. It is often generic ("full sun", "plant in spring"), sometimes contradictory, and rarely connected: the page about a plant says little about its pests, the page about a pest says little about which plants it attacks, and none of it knows anything about *your* garden.

Gardeners end up searching the same questions again and again, and still learn mostly by trial and error.

## Vision statement

> SproutPal is a trustworthy, connected gardening knowledge base for everyone, with garden planning that applies that knowledge to your own plot.

## Who it is for

- **Everyone interested in gardening.** The knowledge base is useful on its own: look up a plant, find out what is eating your tomatoes, check what to do in the garden this month. No account or plot drawing needed.
- **Home gardeners who plan.** People with their own garden or plot who want SproutPal to apply the knowledge to their space: what fits where, and why.

## Principles

1. **Knowledge first.** The knowledge base is the heart of SproutPal, and every recommendation can be traced back to it.
2. **Connected, not scattered.** Plants, care techniques, pests, diseases and seasonal tasks are linked to each other, so one answer leads to the next.
3. **Structured and readable.** Each entry has structured data that the planner can use, and clear text that people can read.
4. **Explain, don't just tell.** Whether in an article or a recommendation, SproutPal says why.
5. **Useful without commitment.** Value comes from the first search; drawing a plot is an extra step for those who want more.

## What success looks like

- People come back to SproutPal when they have a gardening question.
- Answers lead to related knowledge instead of dead ends.
- Gardeners who plan with SproutPal have fewer failed plantings and understand why a plant belongs in a spot.
